# Facilitation Patterns

Practical craft for preparing and running group work — from a 30-minute working meeting to a
multi-day strategy session. The throughline: **the facilitator owns the process, not the
content** — neutral to *what* the group decides, fully responsible for *how* it gets there.

## When to Activate

- Designing a meeting / session / workshop / brainstorm (штурм) / retro
- Writing a session scenario or agenda with timing
- Formulating a session's main question (главный вопрос)
- Choosing a method for analysis, option-generation, or decision
- Preparing the questions to ask a group

## Core Principles

1. **Process over content.** Keep high process control, low content control. You can't steer a
   meeting impartially while invested in its subject — separate the two.
2. **Three circles must balance.** Process (scenario) + rational goal (what to achieve) +
   existential goal (the experience participants leave with).
3. **Scale to the level.** Base meeting (master of the process) → strategy session (process
   architect) → global dialogue (conductor). Match method depth to the level — never over-engineer.
4. **The main question focuses everything.** Open, exploratory, two-part
   (`основная часть + «чтобы что…»`), in the client's language, matched to the pyramid level.
5. **Don't lead with your questions; hold the pause.** The answer lives with the group. After
   asking, the silence belongs to them.
6. **Finish after the room empties.** Capture → distribute → owners → follow-through. No
   after-phase, no result.

## Quick Reference: goals pyramid

| Level | Rational goal | Typical methods |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1. Shared understanding / common field | Align context, build a common picture | brainstorm, moderation, metaphor |
| 2. Search for alternatives | Generate options | 6-3-5, 1-2-4-All, "how might it be", World Café |
| 3. Joint decision | Choose and commit | voting, criteria matrix, "if not my idea, then…" |
| 4. Roadmap | Turn the decision into steps | task table, owners + deadlines |

## Quick Reference: situation type → methods

| Situation type | When | Methods |
|----------------|------|---------|
| Analysis (анализ) | Fragmented/unstructured info; need shared picture | brainstorm, metaphor, skeptics & optimists, SWOT/SOAR, problem tree, Ishikawa, moderation |
| Options (варианты) | Clear problem, enough info to decide | brainstorm, 6-3-5, 1-2-4-All, "how might it be", moderation, World Café, Liberating Structures |
| Choice (выбор) | A list of options exists, pick the best | voting (hands/stickers/dots), sociometry, "if not my idea, then…", criteria-vs-solutions matrix |

When in doubt about depth, prefer the smallest method set that reaches the goal, and alternate
formats (whole group ↔ pairs ↔ small groups ↔ voting) to keep energy and avoid groupthink.
